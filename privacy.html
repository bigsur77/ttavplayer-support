Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TTAVPlayer (“the app”) handles your information. In short: the app is designed to work entirely on your device, and we do not collect your personal data.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no account or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, or location.

Your media and lyrics

Audio, video, and lyrics files you add are stored only on your device. The app does not upload, transmit, or share these files with us or any third party. We have no access to them.

Network use

The app does not stream or download media content, and does not send your files or usage data to any server. The app does not include advertising or third-party tracking.

Purchases

If you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Please see Apple’s Privacy Policy for how Apple handles purchases.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email dev.kawakami@gmail.com.
